# Licensing and attribution policy

CraftLayer's original code and original skill content are licensed under
the MIT License; see the repository's `LICENSE` file.

## Third-party material

References to repositories, documentation, tools, or assets are pointers for
research, not permission to reproduce their material. Before bundling any
third-party code, text, images, fonts, icons, or other assets:

1. Verify the source and the license that applies to the specific material.
2. Confirm that the license permits the intended copying and redistribution.
3. Preserve required copyright notices and attribution.
4. Record the source, license, restrictions, and review date.
5. If rights are unclear, link to the source rather than copying the material.

Do not assume a repository's license covers every asset it references.
Contributors must identify third-party material in their pull requests.
When third-party material is included in a release, maintain
`THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.md` with the applicable notices.

This policy is project guidance, not legal advice.
