# Design quality rules

- Make hierarchy, layout, color, type, motion, and imagery serve user tasks.
- Inspect the existing design system and brand before introducing patterns.
- Treat common patterns as context-dependent, not automatically good or bad.
- Provide realistic content and relevant interaction states.
- Preserve responsive behavior, semantic markup, keyboard access, and
  reduced-motion preferences.
- Never invent product proof, customer logos, metrics, or screenshots.
- Separate objective usability defects from subjective visual preference.
